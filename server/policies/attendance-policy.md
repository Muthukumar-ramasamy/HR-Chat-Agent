# Attendance and Work From Home Policy

Acme Corp (fictional company). Sample policy for demonstration only. Effective 1 Jan 2026.

## 1. Working hours
Standard hours are 9:30 to 18:30, Monday to Friday. Core hours, when everyone must be available, are 11:00 to 16:00.

## 2. Work from home (WFH)
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. WFH is not leave and does not affect leave balances.

## 3. Half-day leave
CL and SL may be taken as half days (0.5 day). EL is taken in full days only.

## 4. Unplanned absence
If you cannot come to work unexpectedly, inform your manager before 10:00 the same day and apply for leave within 2 working days. Otherwise the day is treated as Loss of Pay.
