# Holiday Policy

Acme Corp (fictional company). Sample policy for demonstration only. Effective 1 Jan 2026.

## 1. Holiday calendar
A holiday calendar is published each year for every office location. National holidays apply to all offices. Regional holidays apply only to the office location: Pongal for Chennai, Karnataka Rajyotsava for Bengaluru.

## 2. Holidays on weekends
A holiday that falls on a Saturday or Sunday is not moved to a weekday and no substitute day is given.

## 3. Working on a holiday
Employees asked to work on a public holiday get one compensatory off. It must be taken within 30 days, with manager approval, or it lapses.

## 4. Optional holidays
Each employee may take 2 optional holidays per year from the optional list (festivals not on the main calendar). Optional holidays are not deducted from leave balances.
