# Leave Policy

Acme Corp (fictional company). Sample policy for demonstration only. Effective 1 Jan 2026.

## 1. Scope and leave year
Applies to all full-time employees. The leave year is the calendar year (1 Jan to 31 Dec). Leave types: Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL) and Earned Leave (EL). Annual quotas are credited on 1 January.

## 2. Casual Leave (CL)
12 days per year for personal or urgent matters. At most 3 consecutive working days per request. CL cannot be combined with EL in the same request. Unused CL lapses on 31 December: no carry forward and no encashment.

## 3. Sick Leave (SL)
12 days per year for illness or medical appointments. Absence of more than 2 consecutive working days needs a medical certificate. SL may be applied after returning, within 2 working days. Up to 6 unused days carry forward to the next year. SL is not encashable.

## 4. Earned Leave (EL)
18 days per year for planned time off such as vacations. Available after 6 months of continuous service. Requests of 3 or more days must be applied at least 7 days in advance. Up to 30 unused days carry forward to the next year.

## 5. Leave encashment
Only EL can be encashed: up to 15 days per calendar year, requested in December. Encashed days are deducted from the EL balance.

## 6. New joiners (pro-rata entitlement)
Employees joining during the year get each quota pro-rated: annual quota x remaining months / 12. The joining month counts only if the employee joined on or before the 15th. Results are rounded to the nearest half day. EL still requires 6 months of service before use.

## 7. Loss of Pay (LOP)
Leave taken beyond the available balance, or absence without approved leave, is Loss of Pay. LOP days are unpaid and deducted in the next payroll.

## 8. Weekends and holidays during leave
Weekends and public holidays that fall inside a leave period are not counted as leave days. Only working days are deducted from the balance.

## 9. Applying, approval and cancellation
Apply through HR Assist or the HR portal. The reporting manager approves or rejects. Pending requests reserve balance until decided. Requests can be cancelled before the leave starts; the balance is restored.

## 10. Maternity and paternity leave
Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid, for employees with at least 80 days of service in the past 12 months. Paternity leave: 5 working days, to be used within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
